I work where models meet the systems that have to run them — making things fast enough, reproducible enough, and validated well enough that someone can act on the output.

Right now that means computer vision and forecasting models in production, event-driven pipelines on AWS, and the Terraform underneath them. Before that I spent a summer at AMD benchmarking mixed-precision iterative refinement in C++ against the AOCL-DA libraries, which is where the interest in numerics started.

Selected work

mixed-precision-spectral-clustering — how spectral clustering degrades when the numerics run at reduced precision, and what that buys in runtime.

iterative_refinement_kmeans — mixed-precision iterative refinement applied to K-means, measuring the accuracy-versus-runtime trade-off between single and double precision. Grew out of my research internship at AMD.

Comparative-study-of-RL-Algorithms — reinforcement learning algorithms compared across multi-agent settings under a consistent evaluation protocol.

AlgorTradSystem — regression-based analysis of algorithmic trading strategies. Basis of the research I presented as a BCS Lovelace Colloquium finalist.

scheduler_c — process scheduler written in C.

SymbolDrawingApp — draw-to-search interface for a lens identification system built for an independent optician. The CNN behind it reached 95–96% accuracy.
